Featured Dashboards

Real Power BI dashboard screenshots from my work at Ashland University and Caltrans District 4, covering student housing, degree completion, retention/graduation, program enrollment, and maintenance budget reporting — plus independent practice builds using public/sample data.

Ashland University

Housing — Occupancy vs. Operational Capacity

Built for Ashland's residence life office, this dashboard tracks how full campus housing is relative to operational capacity, term by term. KPI cards up top surface open beds, buyouts, percent occupied, and percent to goal, next to a radial gauge showing current occupancy (1,057 residents) against total capacity (1,496 beds). A bar chart below tracks monthly occupancy against a fixed occupancy goal line, so housing staff can spot a slide toward the goal before it becomes a shortfall. Filters for term, residence hall, room type, gender, class level, and athlete status let staff drill from the university-wide number down to a single hall or population, and a tab strip links out to companion views for capacity detail, occupancy vs. revenue, and billed vs. budgeted revenue.

Ashland University

Degrees Awarded (Unduplicated)

Built for academic affairs, this dashboard tracks how many degrees Ashland has conferred each year, broken out two ways. The top chart shows degrees by level — certificate, associate, bachelor's, master's, and doctorate — across eight academic years, so leadership can see shifts in program mix over time. The bottom chart shows the same degree counts by college (Arts and Sciences, Business and Economics, Education, Nursing and Health Sciences, and the Seminary), surfacing which colleges are driving completions. A footnote flags that current-year totals aren't final until summer degrees are awarded, so viewers don't misread an in-progress year as a decline. Filters for semester, college, academic department, program, degree level and type, undergraduate classification, online status, and correctional education let staff isolate any slice of the completion pipeline.

Ashland University

Retention and Graduation

Also built for academic affairs, this dashboard tracks how well Ashland keeps and graduates its students, cohort over cohort. Headline KPI cards report the current fall-to-fall retention rate (73%) and six-year graduation rate (60%). A retention-rate line chart compares three student bands — first-year to sophomore, sophomore to junior, and junior to senior — across eleven entering cohorts, with tooltips exposing the exact rate behind any point. A stacked graduation-rate bar chart below breaks each cohort's outcome into four-, five-, and six-year completions. A deep filter panel (gender, race/ethnicity, Pell eligibility, first-generation status, residency, financial aid tier, high school GPA, and named programs like Ashbrook and Honors) lets advisors and accreditation reviewers check whether equity gaps are closing, and a companion tab repeats the same view scoped to student-athletes.

Ashland University

Majors Count (as of Census)

Part of a broader majors-and-minors reporting suite, this dashboard gives leadership a census-date view of how many active majors each college carries, and how that number has moved over ten fall terms — from 4,546 up to a peak of 7,078. A left-hand rail lists every college (Arts and Sciences, Business and Economics, Education, Nursing and Health Sciences, Seminary, and Non-Degree) as clickable filters that cross-highlight the trend, alongside dropdown filters for major department, specific major, student level, and correctional education. It sits inside a larger SharePoint-hosted data hub alongside companion pages for minors, admissions funnel, enrollment, credit hours, and degrees — giving staff one place to navigate the full academic-reporting catalog.

Caltrans

FY25/26 District 4 Maintenance — SHA Budget Status

Built as part of my current role as Analyst II at Caltrans District 4, this dashboard tracks State Highway Account (SHA) budget sub-allocation and expenditure status across every maintenance region for the fiscal year. An overview strip totals Operating Expense (OE) and Personnel Services (PS) allocation, encumbrance, and expenditure against the fund, alongside an accounting-period status indicator (AP4 Closed). Two horizontal bar charts break out total OE and PS allocation by region, while two detail tables give budget owners a region-by-region read on allocation vs. expenditure vs. encumbrance, with expense percentage and balance columns conditionally formatted to flag regions running over budget in red. A region slicer and a one-click "Clear All Slicers" control let a budget analyst or program manager drill from the fund-wide total down to a single region in seconds.

Gulf Coast Fuel & Drilling Activity 2024 — Market Intelligence

A self-directed Power BI build modeled on a market-intelligence brief for Retif Oil & Gas, pairing upstream drilling activity with downstream fuel consumption on one page. The left panel tracks 2024 Gulf Coast diesel pricing ($3.46 average) against North American rig counts (787.17 average), with an area chart of weekly diesel prices and a companion line chart and table breaking rig counts out by month. The right panel shifts to U.S. fuel consumption — 137.83 million gallons, 3M thousand barrels, and 16.57K trillion Btu for 2024 — split by state (Texas, California, Florida, New York, North Carolina) in twin bar charts, then further broken into Commercial, Industrial, and Transportation end-use categories via summary tables, a U.S. choropleth map, and a donut chart showing transportation as the dominant consumption category. A state slicer lets a viewer isolate consumption for any single state.
